About BeatDrop

AI-Powered Customer Churn Prediction and Business Explainability Model

Project Overview

Project Name: BeatDrop

Version: Phase 0 - Frontend Mock

Description: An end-to-end solution for predicting customer churn with explainable AI insights to drive retention strategies.

Target Audience: Customer Success Teams, Product Managers, Business Analysts

Problem Statement: Identify at-risk customers before they churn, understand why they might leave, and recommend personalized retention interventions.

Technical Approach: Ensemble machine learning model (XGBoost + Random Forest) with SHAP explainability

Data Sources: Customer usage patterns, support interactions, billing history, contract details

Prediction Horizon: 90-day churn probability

Model Refresh: Daily batch retraining

Key Features

Cohort Analytics

Aggregate views of customer health, churn trends, and business impact across segments

Individual Risk Scoring

Personalized churn probability with root-cause analysis for each customer

What-If Simulation

Interactive scenario modeling to test retention strategies before implementation
