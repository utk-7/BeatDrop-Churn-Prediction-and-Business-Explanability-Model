What is BeatDrop?

Proactive retention intelligence for subscription businesses.

The Challenge of Churn

Losing a subscriber doesn't just cost you next month's revenue—it costs you the entire lifetime value of that customer. Yet most retention efforts are reactive. By the time a customer hits the "Cancel" button, it's already too late.

BeatDrop changes this. Built as an internal analytics and decision-support dashboard, BeatDrop sits on top of a predictive XGBoost machine learning model. Instead of waiting for cancellations, it analyzes streaming behaviors, payment friction, and engagement trends to identify at-risk customers before they leave.

How The Dashboard Works

1. Predict

Identifies exactly which customers are highly likely to cancel their subscriptions in the upcoming billing cycle.

2. Explain

Translates complex machine learning into plain English, showing you exactly why a specific customer is at risk.

3. Value

Estimates the dollar value (Expected Value) of saving them, so you know exactly how much to spend on retention.

4. Act

Suggests the most effective, personalized retention action for your Customer Success team to take immediately.

Built for Customer Success Teams

BeatDrop is designed for the people on the front lines of retention. It doesn't require a data science degree to use. You get actionable intelligence, ranked by business priority, delivered straight to your dashboard.

Data Note: This demonstration is built upon a real, anonymized streaming dataset (KKBox), utilizing a simulated module for listening-behavior metrics. For full technical details regarding data sourcing and assumptions, please refer to the project documentation.
